Privacy

SteadN · steadn.com · last updated 17 August 2026

SteadN is a gardening almanac. This page says plainly what it stores, where it goes, and what it never does. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@steadn.com.

What is kept

  • Your email address, if you sign in. It is how the sign in link reaches you and how your garden is matched to you across devices.
  • Your garden: growing zone, the first three digits of your postal code, bed layouts, plantings, harvest weights, pantry counts and season notes.
  • Questions you ask the advisor, which are sent to Anthropic to be answered.

What is not kept

  • No password. Sign in is a one time link, so there is nothing to steal or reuse.
  • No street address and no precise location. The app asks for a postal code to work out frost dates and only the first three digits are stored.
  • No payment details. If billing is added later it will be handled by a payment processor and card numbers will never reach SteadN.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third party trackers, and no analytics that follow you off this site.

Where it lives

Everything is stored twice on purpose. A copy sits in your own browser so the app works with no signal at all. If you sign in, a second copy sits in a database run by Supabase so the same garden appears on your phone and your computer.

Advisor questions go to Anthropic to be answered and come straight back. They are not stored on our side after the answer is returned.

Row level security in the database means an account can only ever read its own row. This is enforced by the database itself, not by the app, so a bug in the app cannot expose one person's garden to another.

What is shared with other people

Nothing, unless you switch it on. Sharing is off by default. If you do turn it on you choose section by section what a visitor may see, and location is limited to your growing zone or a nearby reference town. Your exact address is never shared at any setting, and neither is your email.

Getting it back or getting rid of it

  • A copy of your data: email hello@steadn.com from your account address.
  • Deletion: ask at the same address and the account and everything in it is removed. There is no waiting period and nothing is retained afterwards.
  • The local copy: clearing site data for steadn.com in your browser removes it from that device.

Children

SteadN is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and anyone with an account is emailed. Quiet edits to a policy page are a bad habit and are not something this project intends to do.
